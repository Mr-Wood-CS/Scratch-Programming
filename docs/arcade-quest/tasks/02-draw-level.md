# Task 2: Make Your Platforms

## Goal

Make a sprite containing the ground and block-style platforms for your first level.

## Watch First

<iframe class="video-frame" src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/CQ4SldacxG4" title="YouTube video about drawing Scratch backdrops" allowfullscreen></iframe>

## Do This

1. Click the **Stage** and then click **Backdrops**.
2. Draw the sky and any scenery. Do not draw the ground or platforms here.
3. In the sprite list below the Stage, click **Choose a Sprite**, then choose **Paint**.
4. Rename the new sprite `Platforms`.
5. In the costume editor, draw a wide ground shape along the bottom.
6. Draw two floating block-style platforms in the same costume.
7. Drag the `Platforms` sprite into position on the Stage.

!!! warning "Keep all platforms in the sprite"
    The ground and floating platforms must be part of the `Platforms` sprite, not the backdrop. This lets the `Player` detect and land on them.

## Check

The sprite list should contain a sprite named `Platforms`. Its costume should contain the ground and two floating platforms, while the backdrop contains only scenery.
