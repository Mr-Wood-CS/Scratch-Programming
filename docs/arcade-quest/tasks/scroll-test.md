# Test The Scrolling Level { .arcade-task }

## Goal

Check that the player looks like they are travelling through a longer level.

## Do This

1. Press the green flag.
2. Hold the right arrow key. Check that the platforms, collectables, enemy, and flag move left.
3. Hold the left arrow key and check that the level moves the other way.
4. Press the green flag again and check that the scrolling position resets.

## Check

- The level sprites move together as the player travels.
- The player looks like they are moving through a longer level.
- Restarting the game resets the scrolling position.
