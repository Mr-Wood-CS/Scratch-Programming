# Task 12: Add Coin Sound

## Goal

Make collecting a coin feel satisfying.

## Do This

1. In the sprite list below the Stage, click the `Coin` sprite. (If you cannot remember where the sprite list is, go back to [Start Here: Where To Click Sprites](../../index.md#where-to-click-sprites).)
2. Click **Sounds**.
3. Click **Choose a Sound**.
4. Choose a short sound from the Scratch sound library.
5. Go back to **Code**.
6. Make sure your collect code includes `play sound coin until done`.
7. Test the game. The coin should make a sound and disappear when collected.
8. Right-click the completed `Coin` sprite in the sprite list and choose **duplicate**.
9. Make several coin copies and drag each one to a different reachable place.

Duplicating the completed sprite copies its costume, sound, and collection code. Do not draw extra coins onto the backdrop.

## Check

Every coin should be a separate sprite. Each one should make a sound, add `1` to the score, disappear when collected, and reappear when the green flag is pressed.
