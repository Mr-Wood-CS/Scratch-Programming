    Task 20: Add A Flag

    Goal

    Add the finish point for the level.

    Do This

    1. Click Choose a Sprite.
    2. Click Paint.
    3. Draw a finish flag.
    4. Rename it Flag.
    5. Put it at the far right of the level.

    Check

    The player should be able to reach the flag after crossing the level.
